# gpa-verify 1.7.2 — video & audio from the Forensic Browser

Forensic Browser 1.6.0 can preserve video and audio (the original media file
as delivered by the platform, or a recording of the playback) together with
the captured pages, under one qualified time-stamp. The media are ordinary
sealed files under `media/NN/`.

## What changes for you

- **ZIP packages and sealed PDFs with embedded media**: nothing — 1.7.1
  already verifies them (the media are covered by `sealed_binding` /
  `attachments` like any other artefact).
- **Sealed PDF whose media were too large to embed** (default limit 100 MB):
  the app delivers a companion archive `…_media.zip` next to the PDF. Run

      gpa-verify GetProofAnchor_sess-….pdf --media GetProofAnchor_sess-…_media.zip

  New check `external_media` compares every media file in the archive with
  the SHA-256 sealed in the PDF. Without `--media` the check is shown as
  skipped and the summary says the media were **not** checked.
- **Plain copy**: a copy that prints the address of the page a video came from
  (when that page was not captured as a page) no longer fails
  `copy_content_consistency`.

## Compatibility

No change for SaaS evidence and for Forensic Browser outputs without media.
Tests: 230 passed, 13 skipped (8 new in `tests/test_release_172.py`, fixtures
in `tests/fixtures/forensic_browser/media/`, generated by the app with its
